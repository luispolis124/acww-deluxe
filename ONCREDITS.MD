# Contributing & Contribution Credits Policy - Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe**! To ensure the project's integrity, proper attribution, and community standards, please follow our contribution and crediting guidelines outlined below.

## 📌 Contribution Rules & Fork Policy

1. **Use Forks for Independent Development:** 
   * Do not directly publish or redistribute standalone modified ROMs or unauthorized forks as official releases. 
   * If you wish to build upon, experiment with, or modify this project, please **Fork** this repository directly on GitHub.

2. **Mandatory Attribution (ONCREDITS):**
   * If you use code, text assets (`.bmg` scripts), patches, or tools from this repository in your own forks or derivative projects, you **must** explicitly credit the original project (**Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe**) and link back to this official repository.
   * Maintain appropriate acknowledgments for all foundational contributors listed in the [`CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md) file.

3. **Submitting Improvements (Pull Requests):**
   * Found a bug, created a better translation string, or optimized a script? We welcome Pull Requests! 
   * Ensure your contributions align with the project's technical scope and do not include any copyrighted ROM binaries.

---
*By contributing to or forking this repository, you agree to respect open-source collaboration etiquette and keep the project ecosystem organized and properly credited.*
