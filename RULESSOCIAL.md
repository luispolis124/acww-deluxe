# Social Media & Content Guidelines - Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe

Thank you for sharing your gameplay, content, and creations featuring **Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe**! To keep our community healthy, legal, and respectful, please follow these guidelines when posting videos, streams, or screenshots on platforms like YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch, and others.

## 📌 Content Creation & Social Rules

1. **No Direct ROM Distribution:**
   * When uploading videos, sharing links, or posting about the mod, **never** link to, host, or distribute pre-patched ROM files or official Nintendo ROM binaries.
   * Always direct your viewers/followers to this official repository or your fork's instructions to obtain the patch legally using their own clean ROMs.

2. **Mandatory Credit & Linkback:**
   * If you showcase gameplay, custom events, or modified text archives (`.bmg`) from this project in your content (videos, reels, shorts, or streams), please mention **Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe** and include a link back to this official repository in your video description or caption.

3. **Respect Intellectual Property:**
   * This mod is a non-profit community project inspired by fans. Respect Nintendo's intellectual property rights regarding original assets, audio, and branding, ensuring your content complies with standard fair use and community guidelines.

4. **Constructive Engagement:**
   * Share your custom villager dialogues, funny moments, and village layouts proudly! Use the community tag or mention your modifications so others can see how you've customized your experience.

---
*Thank you for helping us protect the project, supporting proper distribution practices, and spreading the word about Animal Crossing Wild World Deluxe!*
